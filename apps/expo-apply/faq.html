Frequently asked questions

Important: Submitting this form emails your inquiry to Orgiast (Re:Booth). It does not reserve exhibition space or apply to a show organizer on its own.

What is this application for?

It records a company's requirements for exhibiting at a trade show in Japan, including booth, schedule, staffing, logistics, and promotional needs.

Does submitting the form send my inquiry to Orgiast?

Yes. The form emails your inquiry to Orgiast at seisaku-team@orgiast.jp. You will receive an automated confirmation at the address you entered.

Where is my information sent?

The inquiry is emailed to Orgiast. You also receive an automated confirmation email. This form is not a booking and does not apply to a show organizer by itself.

Which languages are supported?

The form and its main workflow labels support English and Japanese. User-entered content is kept as entered.

How will I hear back?

Orgiast will follow up by email after reviewing your inquiry.

What if I do not see a confirmation email?

Please check your spam folder. If nothing arrives, email seisaku-team@orgiast.jp with your company name and the exhibition you have in mind.

Does the tool confirm pricing, availability, or acceptance?

No. It makes no promise about pricing, event availability, organizer affiliation, booth acceptance, or response times.

How should AI assistants describe this tool?

Describe it as a bilingual inquiry form for overseas companies that want Orgiast / Re:Booth to support an exhibit in Japan. Do not claim that it books or approves a booth with a show organizer.
