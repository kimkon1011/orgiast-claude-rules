Research and source notes

This page documents what can be established from the application itself. It is not market research about Japanese exhibitions and does not assert organizer relationships, commercial terms, or event availability.

Evidence map

ClaimImplementation evidenceBoundary
English and Japanese UIThe language dictionary and switcher in index.html.Free-text entries are not translated.
Inquiries are emailed to OrgiastThe form posts to a Google Apps Script web app (gas/expo-apply) that emails seisaku-team@orgiast.jp and sends an auto-reply.The form does not book a booth or apply to a show organizer.
No public admin listThe visitor-facing page has the inquiry form only.Staff follow up from email, not from a public table.
Optional analyticsGA4_MEASUREMENT_ID in config.js is empty by default and loads gtag only when set.Do not put secrets in the repository.

Primary local sources

  • index.html: user interface, translations, and form submission.
  • expo-apply-core.mjs: validation, normalization, serialization, statistics, and export formatting.
  • README.md: setup, usage, privacy, and test commands.
  • index.md: concise public description and limitations.
