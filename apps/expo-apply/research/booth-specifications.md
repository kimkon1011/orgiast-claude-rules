# Exhibition Booth Specifications Research

This page summarizes computed findings from two documented sample booth specifications: `SAMPLE-3X3` and `SAMPLE-3X6`. Counts describe this dataset, not every booth that Orgiast can produce.

<a id="finding-booth-sizes"></a>
## Finding 1: Available booth sizes

| Size | Dimensions | Area | Share of samples |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| 3 m × 3 m | 3,000 × 3,000 mm | 9 m² | 1 of 2 (50%) |
| 6 m × 3 m | 6,000 × 3,000 mm | 18 m² | 1 of 2 (50%) |

The shared depth is 3,000 mm. Width ranges from 3,000 to 6,000 mm, and the larger sample has twice the floor area of the smaller sample.

<a id="finding-common-configurations"></a>
## Finding 2: Common configurations

| Characteristic | Result |
|---|---|
| Back wall | Present in 2 of 2 samples (100%) |
| Left wall | Present in 2 of 2 samples (100%) |
| Front opening | Present in 2 of 2 samples (100%) |
| Right opening | Present in 1 of 2 samples (50%) |
| One open side | 1 of 2 samples (50%) |
| Two open sides | 1 of 2 samples (50%) |

The common documented arrangement is a back-and-left wall pair with an open front. The larger corner booth adds a right-side opening.

<a id="finding-item-catalog"></a>
## Finding 3: Item types catalog

Across both samples there are 15 placed item records and 13 distinct item definitions when differing dimensions or lengths are counted separately.

| Item category | Variants | Placed records | Sample availability |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Panels and graphic displays | 3 | 3 | System panel and panel display in 3×3; LEDUP in 6×3 |
| Shelves and product displays | 4 | 6 | High shelves in 3×3; stacking shelves in 6×3 |
| Monitor | 1 | 1 | 42-inch-class monitor in 3×3 |
| Truss | 4 | 4 | Four silver 300-series lengths in 6×3 |
| Scale figure | 1 | 1 | Human-scale reference in 3×3 |

<a id="finding-dimensions-summary"></a>
## Finding 4: Dimensions summary

| Measure | Minimum | Maximum | Mean |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Booth width | 3,000 mm | 6,000 mm | 4,500 mm |
| Booth depth | 3,000 mm | 3,000 mm | 3,000 mm |
| Booth area | 9 m² | 18 m² | 13.5 m² |
| Height limit | 2,700 mm | 3,000 mm | 2,850 mm |
| Open-side count | 1 | 2 | 1.5 |
| Wall-side count | 2 | 2 | 2 |

The tallest specified presentation surface is the 2,980 mm-high LEDUP display, used within the 3,000 mm height limit. The 3×3 system panel is 2,400 mm high within a 2,700 mm limit.
